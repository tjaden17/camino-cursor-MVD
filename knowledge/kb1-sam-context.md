# Sam — user context (CSM)

## Role

**Customer Success Manager** at Locumate.

## Background

Second product startup; roughly **five years** in account management, customer success, and CSM roles.

## Primary goals

- **Show customer value** using data.
- **Improve trust** to support **retention and upsell**.
- Turn **adoption insights** into **actions**.

## Key decisions and timing

| Decision | When |
|----------|------|
| Account health actions | Ongoing |
| Renewal and upsell strategy | Before renewals |
| Meeting preparation | Before each customer meeting |

## Metrics she cares about

- **signups**
- **shifts**
- **jobs_posted**
- **job_seekers**
- **shift_utilisation**
- **adoption_by_segment**

## Also watches

- **inactive_pharmacy_rate**
- **unsubscribed_pharmacy_rate**
- **prospect_opportunity_size**

## Pain points

- **Manual reporting** takes a lot of time.
- **Timing differences** across sources create **reconciliation** work.
- **Missing filters** in reports limit usefulness.
- **Low trust** when stakeholders cannot **verify** figures.

## Trust requirements

- **Clear metric definitions**.
- **Calculation traceability**.
- Ability to **verify against source data**.
