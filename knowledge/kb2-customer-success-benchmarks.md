# Customer success and SaaS retention benchmarks (retrieval notes)

Reference notes for **churn**, **NRR/NDR**, **NPS**, **renewals**, and **expansion**. SaaS metrics are **definition-sensitive** (logo vs. revenue churn; **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)** vs. **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**).

## Churn rates by company stage (revenue vs. logo)

**ChartMogul** analyzes **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** across **>2,500 SaaS businesses** and describes **churn and contraction** dynamics by **ARR scale**, noting **churn’s contribution to ARR lost** can rise for companies **below ~$3M ARR** following prior **growth surges** and macro tightening (*ChartMogul*, *The SaaS Retention Report: The New Normal For SaaS*, 2024).

**OpenView** & **High Alpha**’s *2024 SaaS Benchmarks Report* provides **ARR-band growth** context (e.g., stabilization patterns post-2021) useful for **stage-appropriate** expectations (*OpenView* & *High Alpha*, *2024 SaaS Benchmarks Report*, 2024).

**Gainsight** publishes **churn analytics** research based on large samples (**100,000+ data points** advertised for churn warning signals) (*Gainsight*, *The State of Customer Churn in 2024 Report*—**Gainsight**, 2024).

**Caution:** free summaries rarely give a single **“seed churn = X%”** table; prefer **NRR/GRR** with **cohort definitions** (*ChartMogul*, 2024; *Gainsight*, 2024).

## NPS ranges and what they indicate

**NPS** measures **recommendation likelihood** on a 0–10 scale with **Promoters/Passives/Detractors** grouping (**Bain & Company** Net Promoter System methodology). **NPS** is widely tracked in CS programs (*Gainsight*, *The 2024 Customer Success Index*, 2024—NPS among benchmarked CS KPIs).

**Interpretation:** absolute **NPS** differs by **industry**, **survey channel**, and **sampling bias**; trend and **verbatim themes** often matter more than a single number (*Gainsight*, *2024 Customer Success Index*; *Bain & Company*, Net Promoter System reference materials).

## Health score frameworks

**Health scores** combine **usage**, **support**, **NPS/CSAT**, **billing**, and **relationship** signals. **Gainsight**’s *2024 Customer Success Index* documents **CS function maturity** and **digital tooling** adoption (e.g., **digital CS tools** adoption reported **73%** in 2024 vs. **42%** in 2023 in press summaries of the index) (*Gainsight*, press release / *2024 Customer Success Index*, 2024).

**Framework note:** health scoring is **vendor- and motion-specific**; benchmarks are **internal calibration** first (*Gainsight*, 2024).

## Renewal rates and gross retention

**GRR** and **renewal rate** reflect **downsell/churn** excluding expansion; **NRR** includes **expansion**. **ChartMogul** emphasizes **NRR** as a **primary growth lever** when new business is harder (*ChartMogul*, *SaaS Retention Report*, 2024).

**Empirical pattern (ChartMogul, 2024):** **top quartile** firms with **≤1.5K subscribers** reached **~100% NRR** in **H1 2024** in their sample, while **>12K subscribers** often clustered around **~76% NRR**—illustrating **scale effects** on retention outcomes (*ChartMogul*, *SaaS Retention Report*, 2024).

## Expansion revenue benchmarks

**ChartMogul** reports **expansion** contributing **~40% of growth** for companies with **$15M–30M+ ARR** by 2024 vs. **~30%** in early 2021 at prior growth peak (*ChartMogul*, *SaaS Retention Report*, 2024). **Median firms with ≥100% NRR** grew **~48% YoY**—roughly **double** firms in lower NRR bands in their analysis (*ChartMogul*, *SaaS Retention Report*, 2024).

**Bessemer** public materials discuss **cloud/AI** efficiency and scaling patterns for modern software businesses—useful **strategic context** alongside **NRR** planning (*Bessemer Venture Partners*, *State of the Cloud 2024*).

## CS organization benchmarks (leading indicators of maturity)

**Gainsight**’s **2024** index materials report high **CS adoption** in B2B tech and rising **CS Ops** prevalence (e.g., **>50%** of B2B tech firms with **dedicated CS Ops** in summarized findings) (*Gainsight*, *The 2024 Customer Success Index*). **Gainsight**’s **2025** index (“Outcomes Over Everything”) highlights **AI** embedded in CS workflows (**>50%** of companies integrating **AI**) and continued emphasis on **NRR** alongside adoption/expansion metrics (*Gainsight*, *Customer Success Index 2025: Outcomes Over Everything*, 2025—per *Gainsight* public summaries). **AI in CS** adoption is also tracked in **Gainsight**’s **2024** AI report (*Gainsight*, *The State of AI in Customer Success, 2024 Report*).

## Practical benchmarking checklist

1. **Define churn type:** **logo**, **revenue**, **voluntary vs. involuntary** (*ChartMogul*, churn discussion, 2024).  
2. **Pair NRR with GRR** to separate **expansion quality** from **retention quality** (*ChartMogul*, 2024).  
3. **Segment by ARPA and subscriber count**—**NRR difficulty** rises with scale in *Chartmogul*’s sample (*ChartMogul*, 2024).  
4. **Use CS instrumentation maturity** as context (*Gainsight*, 2024).
