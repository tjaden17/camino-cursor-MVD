# Locumate — company context

## Summary

Locumate is an early-stage B2B workforce technology company in the pharmacy and healthcare space, delivered as SaaS.

## Industry and model

- **Industry:** Workforce tech for pharmacies and healthcare; product is SaaS.
- **Business model:** B2B (sells to organizations, not consumers).
- **Stage:** Seed.

## Team

Roughly **17 people**. **14** appear in Zoho Users, spanning roles such as CEO, COO, three in Customer Success, Marketing, Product, and Sales.

## Product

A **platform that connects pharmacies with locum (temporary) pharmacists** so shifts can be staffed.

## Connected data sources

| Source | Typical use |
|--------|-------------|
| Zoho CRM | Leads, Deals |
| Zoho Desk | Tickets, Accounts, Agents |
| Shifts Data | Operational shift records |
| Zoho Users | Internal user directory |

## Strategic context

Locumate is **early-stage**. The **CEO** and **CSM** need **cross-functional visibility** across sales, support, and operations so decisions are grounded in the same picture of the business.
