# Analysis checklists by KPI type

Use these as **ordered prompts** for breakdowns before synthesizing. Each step should cite **computed** slices from the data.

## support_issue_volume

### Checklist

1. **Total volume and trend** — level vs prior period; week-over-week or month-over-month as defined.
2. **Channel** — Phone, Email, Chat (and any other Desk channels).
3. **Category / classification** — ticket type, product area, or Desk fields in use.
4. **Resolution time trend** — median or mean time to resolve vs baseline.
5. **SLA compliance** — met vs breached share if SLAs exist in data.
6. **FCR (first contact resolution)** — rate trend if tracked.
7. **Synthesis driver** — which **dimension** best explains the change vs last period?

---

## win_rate

### Checklist

1. **Overall rate and trend** — win rate and direction over time.
2. **Deal size tier** — small / medium / large buckets from Amount.
3. **Sales cycle length** — time from create to close by bucket.
4. **Pipeline coverage** — open pipeline vs targets (if available in same stack).
5. **Stage appropriateness** — does the win rate pattern match **stage** and **cycle** expectations?

---

## shifts and shift_utilisation

### Checklist

1. **Total volume and trend** — finished shifts (and posted/created if relevant).
2. **Pharmacy group** — by group / banner.
3. **State** — geographic breakdown if present in data.
4. **Seasonality** — same period prior year or rolling baseline.
5. **Synthesis** — **utilisation** direction plus **capacity** implications (under/over use vs expected hours).

---

## prospects

### Checklist

1. **Total count and trend** — new leads in period.
2. **Month** — within-period monthly distribution.
3. **Conversion status** — converted vs not, if status fields exist.
4. **Lead source** — source or campaign distribution.
5. **Synthesis** — is **lead generation** keeping pace with goals or prior periods?

---

## pipeline_value

### Checklist

1. **Total value and trend** — open pipeline sum over time.
2. **Stage** — value by pipeline stage.
3. **Deal owner** — concentration and changes by owner.
4. **Average deal size** — mean or median open deal Amount.
5. **Synthesis** — **pipeline coverage** vs **target** or internal benchmark if defined.
