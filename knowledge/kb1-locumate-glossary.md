# Locumate — domain glossary

## Shift

A **job placement** a pharmacy needs filled by a locum (one staffing need on the platform).

## Locum

A **temporary staff member** (pharmacist) who fills shifts at pharmacies.

## Agency usage

When a pharmacy fills staffing needs via an **external staffing agency** instead of a **direct Locumate platform** fill.

## Groups

A **collection of pharmacies** under one umbrella brand or banner. Same concept as **pharmacy group**.

## Pharmacy group

Same as **groups** — pharmacies grouped under a parent brand.

## Signal to insight

**Time** from detecting a **metric change** to having an **understood cause** (interpretation lag).

## Fill rate

**Percentage** of **posted shifts** that end up **filled**.

## Shift utilisation

**Hours worked** divided by **hours expected** (capacity usage for completed work), typically over finished shifts.
