# Surge — user context (CEO)

## Role

**CEO** of Locumate.

## Primary goals

- Improve decisions with **cross-functional visibility**.
- **Prioritize the product roadmap** using **evidence**.
- **Reduce signal-to-insight time** (faster from “something moved” to “why”).

## Key decisions and horizon

| Decision | Horizon |
|----------|---------|
| Product roadmap priorities | ~90 days |
| Hiring vs outsourcing | ~90 days |
| Market expansion | ~6 months |

## Metrics he cares about

- **prospects**
- **calls_per_week**
- **win_rate**
- **support_issue_volume**
- **churn_leading_indicator**
- **bug_impact**

## Also watches

- **platform_usage**
- **onboarding_quality**
- **agency_usage_change**

## Pain points

- Slow path from **signal** to **explanation**.
- **Limited confidence** when interpreting metrics **across functions**.
- Hard to **link customer behavior** to **concrete action**.

## Trust requirements

- **Source-level traceability** (numbers tie back to systems).
- **Calculation explainability** (how a metric was derived).
- **Cross-team consistency** (same definitions and logic everywhere).
